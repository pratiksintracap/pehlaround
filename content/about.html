What we are, and are not

A deal desk that introduces registered family offices to private companies and shareholders, builds the deal room and documents the transaction. Not a stock exchange, trading platform, broker, merchant banker, research analyst or investment adviser, and not registered with SEBI or the RBI. Where a deal needs a registered intermediary, such as a merchant banker for an IPO, the room names it.

What it costs a family office

Nothing: not to register, not to open a room, not to commit.

For companies

The desk takes profitable companies with a stated listing plan that meet our tests: not carried elsewhere, revenue and profit today, demonstrably IPO-bound, priced below its listed peers, and the promoter keeps the majority. List your company.
