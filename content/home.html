The product

The product, end to end.From the shelf to allotment.

The desk does the heavy lifting before any office sees a company, and runs every step after you say yes. Click any step, or let it play.

Sourcing

The desk finds IPO-bound companies before any banker or unlisted platform is marketing them, and deals directly with the promoter.

What you see: Nothing yet. This is the heavy lifting you never have to do.

Primary diligence

Meetings with the promoter and the management team, plant and site visits, customer verifications and a read of the competitive landscape, all before a single office is approached.

What you see: What the desk found, and the risks it sees, in the room.

Valuation and plans

The desk sets up the valuation against listed peers, reviews the growth plan, the business and the listing plan, and guides the promoter on what the round and the IPO path need. Same terms for every office, lock-in stated.

What you see: The terms, the valuation basis and the peer set, in the Terms panel.

Diligence checks

A standard set of checks for every deal, from the memorandum, audits and order book to title, promoter background, references and valuation, plus more wherever the company, its sector or its history calls for them. Risks are written before the numbers.

What you see: The tracker with dates and statuses, the risks, and what is still pending.

Room built

Every data-room document in place before the first office is contacted: memorandum, financials, shareholding, title and contracts. Snapshot, multiples, terms, IPO path, diligence, Q&A and a one-page deal summary, in the same layout for every deal.

What you see: All of it, the moment your NDA is signed.

Close

When the round subscribes, the desk completes the closing with the company or seller and keeps reporting to every office until the listing.

What you see: Closing status and updates, in the room and your dashboard.

Register

One form: name, office, email, mobile, role and how the office invests. No PAN or ID to look at deals. No fee, now or ever.

What you see: Every deal overview opens immediately.

Sign the NDA

An NDA between your office and the company, signed on screen with your name and an emailed code. The company's room opens straight away.

What you see: That company's name, numbers and documents.

Open the room

The room opens the moment that company's NDA is signed. Read the risks first, then the numbers, then the tracker. Nobody from the desk calls you.

What you see: Everything the desk has: every panel, every document, every question other offices asked.

Promoter calls

The desk sets up calls with the promoter and the management team, as group sessions under the NDA, and posts a written summary for every office. Questions can also go in writing.

What you see: Every answer, with who gave it and when.

Term sheet and diligence

Indicate an amount and the desk shares the draft term sheet and shareholders' agreement. Independent due diligence by a trusted firm can be run before you sign. You confirm on a summary of terms and risks after KYC, or pass with a one-line reason.

What you see: The drafts, the diligence report and your allocation, in the dashboard.

Compliance and allotment

The desk runs the paperwork: escrow account, board and shareholder resolutions with MGT-14, the private placement offer letter and the PAS-3 return of allotment, or the share transfer for a secondary. Money moves to the company or seller through escrow.

What you see: Shares credited to the family's demat, then quarterly updates and lock-in dates in the dashboard.

How the money moves

Your money goes straight to the company.It never passes through the desk.

The desk finds, checks and documents the deal. The investment goes from you to the company or seller under signed documents, and the shares come to your demat. The desk never holds investors' money.
