We are a deal desk that introduces family offices, funds and qualified investors to private companies raising capital. We are not a stock exchange, trading platform, broker, merchant banker or investment adviser, and we are not registered with SEBI.

No offer. Nothing on this website is an offer or invitation to subscribe for or buy securities. Any offer is made privately, only to identified investors, through a private placement offer letter under the Companies Act, 2013.

Information. Company information is provided by the companies and checked by the desk on a best-effort basis from the sources stated. It may contain errors or omissions, and projections may not be achieved.

No advice. We do not give investment, legal or tax advice or recommendations. Each investor decides on the documents and its own advisers.

Risk. Investments in unlisted companies are illiquid, may not list on the expected timeline or at all, and can lose their entire value.

Eligibility. This site is for family offices, funds (AIFs, PMS and similar), individual investors who are accredited or invest at least ₹25 lakh per company, and their authorised representatives only.
