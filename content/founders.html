Would your round get a room?

Indicative only. The desk's tests, as questions. Yes to all of them and the intake call is a formality; otherwise the desk will tell you what to fix first.

  • Revenue and positive PAT today, audited or in audit?
  • Would you price the round below where your listed peers trade?
  • A main-board or SME listing planned within 36 months?
  • No banker-marketed round, and not on any unlisted platform?
  • Promoter keeps the majority through the round and the listing?
