Promoter sessions

The promoter, across the table, every week

A founder or CFO walks registered family offices through one deal on the desk: the business, the numbers, the raise, and your questions. Small groups, so the questions are yours. Recordings stay on the desk.

Format

One deal, a few offices

The promoter presents, then the desk and the room ask their questions. No slides you have not already seen in the data room. Every registered office gets the same session and the same written summary.

Who attends

Registered family offices only

Principals, CIOs and the office's advisers. The schedule, joining links and recordings are published inside the desk, not on this page, because these are private placements.

After the session

Request an allocation from the room

Indicate an amount in the room. It is non-binding; a commitment is made only on signed documents after KYC, with a summary of terms and risks to confirm.
